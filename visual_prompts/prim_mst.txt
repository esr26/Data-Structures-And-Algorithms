When to Use
Use to connect all vertices of a weighted undirected graph at minimum total cost.

How It Works
Example: Triangle AB=1, BC=2, AC=4: MST cost is 3.
1. Start from A with cost 0.
2. Push candidate incident edges into a min-heap.
3. Take smallest edge reaching an unvisited vertex: AB.
4. Next take BC.
5. Stop after every vertex joins the tree.

Quick Facts
O(E log V) time with a heap, O(V+E) storage. Pitfall: skipping visited vertices prevents cycles; disconnected graphs lack a full MST.
