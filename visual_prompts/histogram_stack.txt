When to Use
Use to find the widest span where each bar can be the minimum height.

How It Works
Example: Bars [2,1,2] have maximum rectangle area 3.
1. Keep indices of increasing bar heights.
2. At shorter height 1, pop height 2 and compute its width.
3. Push the new bar.
4. At the end, pop remaining bars using a zero sentinel.
5. Track maximum area 3.

Quick Facts
O(n) time, O(n) space. Pitfall: compute width from the new stack top after a pop.
