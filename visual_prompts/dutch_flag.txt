When to Use
Use to sort arrays containing only 0, 1, and 2 in place.

How It Works
Example: Transform [2,0,1] into [0,1,2].
1. Place low at left, mid at left, high at right.
2. At mid=2, swap with high and decrease high.
3. At mid=1, advance mid.
4. At mid=0, swap with low and advance both.
5. Stop when mid passes high.

Quick Facts
O(n) time, O(1) space. Pitfall: after swapping a 2, inspect the new mid value again.
