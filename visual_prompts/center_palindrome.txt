When to Use
Use to find palindromic substrings by symmetry around a center.

How It Works
Example: In 'babad', 'bab' is a longest palindrome.
1. Try each character as an odd-length center.
2. Also try each gap as an even-length center.
3. Expand while both outer characters match.
4. Record the longest span seen.
5. Return its substring.

Quick Facts
O(n²) time, O(1) extra space. Pitfall: include both odd and even centers.
