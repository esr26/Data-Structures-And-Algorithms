When to Use
Use when negative values can flip the best product into the worst and back.

How It Works
Example: For [2,3,-2,4], maximum product is 6.
1. Track both the maximum and minimum product ending here.
2. At 3, maximum becomes 6.
3. At -2, consider products from both previous extremes.
4. Update the global maximum at every step.
5. Return 6.

Quick Facts
O(n) time, O(1) space. Pitfall: compute new min and max from old values before overwriting.
