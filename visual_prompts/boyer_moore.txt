When to Use
Use to find candidates occurring more than n/2 or n/3 times.

How It Works
Example: For [2,2,1,2], candidate 2 occurs three times.
1. Keep a candidate and counter.
2. Increase for matching values; decrease for others.
3. Replace the candidate when the counter reaches zero.
4. Count the final candidate to verify the threshold.

Quick Facts
O(n) time, O(1) space. Pitfall: a candidate is not guaranteed to be a majority without verification.
