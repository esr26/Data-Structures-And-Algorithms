When to Use
Use to count inversions or reverse pairs while sorting.

How It Works
Example: For [3,1,2], inversions are (3,1) and (3,2): total 2.
1. Split into sorted halves.
2. Count pairs inside each half recursively.
3. During merge, count cross pairs before merging.
4. Merge halves into sorted order.
5. Add all counts.

Quick Facts
O(n log n) time, O(n) space. Pitfall: inversion and reverse-pair inequalities differ.
