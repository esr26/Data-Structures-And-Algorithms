When to Use
Use to find the next warmer day or previous greater item efficiently.

How It Works
Example: Temperatures [30,40,35] give waits [1,0,0].
1. Store unresolved indices in a decreasing-temperature stack.
2. Read 40 and pop 30's index.
3. Set its wait to the index difference 1.
4. Push 40, then 35.
5. Leave unresolved indices at zero.

Quick Facts
O(n) time, O(n) space. Pitfall: store indices when distances are needed.
