How It Works
Example: Merge [1,3] and [2,4] into [1,4].

2. Start the output with [1,3].
3. Compare [2,4] to the last merged end.
4. Extend the end to 4 on overlap.
5. Append intervals that do not overlap.

Quick Facts
O(n log n) time for sorting, O(n) output space. Pitfall: decide whether touching endpoints count as overlap.
