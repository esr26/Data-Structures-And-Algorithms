When to Use
Use when comparing neighboring values reveals an uphill direction toward a peak.

How It Works
Example: For [1,3,2], index 1 is a peak.
1. Check middle and its right neighbor.
2. If rising, keep the right half.
3. Otherwise keep the left half including middle.
4. When low equals high, return that peak.

Quick Facts
O(log n) time, O(1) space. Pitfall: compare within bounds and know whether strict peaks are required.
