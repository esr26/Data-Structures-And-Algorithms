When to Use
Use for permutations or longest substring with limited replacements.

How It Works
Example: Find an anagram of 'ab' inside 'eidbaooo': substring 'ba'.
1. Count required characters of 'ab'.
2. Move a length-2 window over the text.
3. Add the entering character and remove the leaving one.
4. Compare window counts with target counts.
5. Report the matching 'ba'.

Quick Facts
O(n) time with fixed alphabet; O(k) frequency space. Pitfall: keep counts in sync at both window boundaries.
