Use to list all divisors without testing every number through n.

How It Works
Example: Divisors of 12 include pairs (1,12), (2,6), (3,4).
1. Test i from 1 through floor(sqrt 12).
2. When 12 mod i is zero, add i.
3. Add paired divisor 12/i if different.
4. Sort results if ordered output is required.

Quick Facts
O(sqrt n) tests; output storage O(number of divisors). Pitfall: do not duplicate square-root divisor for perfect squares.
