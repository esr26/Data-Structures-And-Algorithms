When to Use
Use to find the shortest substring containing all required characters with multiplicity.

How It Works
Example: In 'ADOBECODEBANC' covering 'ABC', answer is 'BANC'.
1. Expand right, counting required characters.
2. When all needs are met, record the window.
3. Move left while coverage remains valid.
4. Restore a missing requirement when left moves too far.
5. Continue and keep the shortest valid window.

Quick Facts
O(n) time, O(k) map space. Pitfall: repeated required characters demand counts, not set membership.
