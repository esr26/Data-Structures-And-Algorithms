When to Use
Use when only a recent survivor can interact with the next moving item.

How It Works
Example: Asteroids [5,10,-5] leave [5,10].
1. Scan asteroids left to right.
2. Push right-moving 5 and 10.
3. Incoming -5 meets top 10.
4. Discard -5 because 10 is larger.
5. Keep prior survivors.

Quick Facts
O(n) time, O(n) space. Pitfall: only a right-mover before a left-mover can collide.
