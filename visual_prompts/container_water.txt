When to Use
Use to maximize width times the shorter of two boundary heights.

How It Works
Example: For heights [1,3,2,4], endpoints 3 and 4 enclose area 6.
1. Start pointers at both ends.
2. Compute width times minimum endpoint height.
3. Record the best area.
4. Move the pointer at the shorter height inward.
5. Repeat until pointers meet.

Quick Facts
O(n) time, O(1) space. Pitfall: moving the taller side cannot improve the current height limit.
