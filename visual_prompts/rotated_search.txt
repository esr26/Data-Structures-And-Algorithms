When to Use
Use when an ascending array was rotated once and one side remains sorted.

How It Works
Example: Find 1 in [4,5,1,2,3].
1. Inspect middle element.
2. Determine which half is sorted.
3. Check whether target lies in that sorted half.
4. Discard the other half.
5. Repeat until found or empty.

Quick Facts
O(log n) with distinct values; duplicates can force O(n). Pitfall: duplicate ends obscure the sorted-half test.
