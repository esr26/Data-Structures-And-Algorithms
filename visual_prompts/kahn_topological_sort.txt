When to Use
Use to order a directed acyclic graph by prerequisites.

How It Works
Example: With A->B and A->C, A must appear before B and C.
1. Count indegrees.
2. Enqueue all zero-indegree nodes, starting with A.
3. Remove A and emit it.
4. Decrease B and C indegrees.
5. Enqueue newly zero nodes and finish.

Quick Facts
O(V+E) time, O(V) space. Pitfall: fewer than V emitted nodes signals a directed cycle.
