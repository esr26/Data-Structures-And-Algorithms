When to Use
Use to test whether two sequences have a one-to-one correspondence.

How It Works
Example: 'egg' maps to 'add' with e->a and g->d.
1. Walk characters in parallel.
2. Record the forward mapping e->a.
3. Record the reverse mapping a->e.
4. Check every later pair against both maps.
5. Reject any mismatch or collision.

Quick Facts
O(n) expected time, O(k) map space. Pitfall: one-way mapping permits two source symbols to map to one target.
