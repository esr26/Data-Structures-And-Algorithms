When to Use
Use capacity-constrained pairing where extremes help decide feasible partners.

How It Works
Example: Weights [1,2,2], limit 3 use boats (1,2) and (2).
1. Sort the weights.
2. Point to lightest and heaviest.
3. Pair them if their sum fits.
4. Otherwise send the heaviest alone.
5. Count a boat and repeat.

Quick Facts
O(n log n) time, O(1) extra space apart from sort. Pitfall: the two-person limit is essential to this greedy rule.
