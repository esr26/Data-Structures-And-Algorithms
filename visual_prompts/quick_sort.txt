When to Use
Use an in-place comparison sort where average speed is useful.

How It Works
Example: Sort [3,1,2] around pivot 2 into [1,2,3].
1. Choose a pivot.
2. Partition values less and greater around it.
3. Place pivot in its final position.
4. Recursively sort left and right partitions.
5. Combine implicitly in place.

Quick Facts
Average O(n log n), worst O(n²); recursion O(log n) average, O(n) worst. Pitfall: poor pivots on ordered input.
