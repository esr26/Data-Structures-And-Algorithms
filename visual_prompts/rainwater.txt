When to Use
Use to sum water trapped between elevation bars.

How It Works
Example: For [2,0,2], the middle traps 2 units.
1. Set pointers at both ends and track left/right maxima.
2. Advance the side with the smaller boundary.
3. Update its maximum.
4. Add max minus current height when below that maximum.
5. Continue until pointers cross.

Quick Facts
O(n) time, O(1) space. Pitfall: water depends on the smaller enclosing maximum.
