When to Use
Use to find median or kth element without merging arrays.

How It Works
Example: Median of [1,3] and [2] is 2.
1. Binary search a cut in the shorter array.
2. Choose the complementary cut in the other array.
3. Compare left maxima with opposite right minima.
4. Move the cut until both cross inequalities hold.
5. Read the median or kth boundary.

Quick Facts
O(log min(m,n)) time, O(1) space. Pitfall: handle empty-side sentinels and odd/even totals.
