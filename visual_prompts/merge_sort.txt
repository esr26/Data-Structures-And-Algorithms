How It Works
Example: Sort [3,1,2] by merging [1] and [2] then [3].
1. Split the list into halves.
2. Recursively sort each half.
3. Compare front elements of both halves.
4. Emit the smaller and advance.
5. Append the remainder.

Quick Facts
O(n log n) time, O(n) auxiliary space for arrays. Pitfall: copy remaining elements after one side empties.
