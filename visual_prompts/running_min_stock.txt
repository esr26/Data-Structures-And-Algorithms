When to Use
Use when a later value is compared with the lowest earlier value.

How It Works
Example: Prices [7,1,5,3] give max profit 4 from 1 to 5.
1. Start minimum price at 7.
2. At 1, update the minimum.
3. At 5, compute profit 5-1=4.
4. At 3, compare profit with best.
5. Return the maximum nonnegative profit.

Quick Facts
O(n) time, O(1) space. Pitfall: sale must occur after purchase.
